# Changelog

All notable changes to the CAPTURE Framework are recorded here.

Versioning: a **major** version changes the stages or the core principles; a **minor** version
adds or reworks sections without changing the structure; a **patch** fixes wording and errors.

## 1.0 — November 2026

First public release.

- The framework in two editions: English (`CAPTURE-Framework.md`) and Russian
  (`CAPTURE-Framework.ru.md`). Text finalized in August 2026.
- Seven stages: Connect, Apply, Prove, Team Scale, Unify, Restrict & Govern, Expand.
- Architecture of Agentic Analytics, operating principles, expected outcomes and the link to
  AI-native transformation.
- Designed editions: the English PDF as a release asset, and both editions readable online
  at https://1even-lab.github.io/capture-framework/ (`/en/` and `/ru/`).
